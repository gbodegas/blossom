How Blossom uses your information {# The link in the plan panel points at this paragraph rather than the disclosure around it, so a browser that reveals the target's fold on a fragment jump opens it; the script does the same on the click. #}

Planning takes a minute or two. It sends your week, the household's rules, the planner's notes about its past plans, your updates of Not yet on that work with any note you wrote, and, when you have said today is too much, that you said so, to the model provider; when the first plan needs another try, the plan it made and what was wrong with it go too. Work you report as Done is left out, note and all. Nothing else leaves.

Your updates and notes are kept in the family's own file. Your parents can read them on the family page. Done means you have finished your part; it does not turn work in, and your school record is separate.

"Too much right now" takes one press, no rating, no reason, and you can take it back.
